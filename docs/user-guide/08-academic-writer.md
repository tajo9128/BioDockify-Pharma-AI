# Chapter 8: Academic Writer

## 8.1 Overview
The Academic Writer is an 8-tab writing suite for research papers, theses, grants, regulatory documents, citations, lectures, and slides.

### Access Path
**All Tools → Academic Writer** (or click 📖 icon)

### Tabs: Lit Review · Paper · Thesis · Grant · Regulatory · Citations · Lecture · Slides

---

## 8.2 Literature Review Tab
Generate systematic literature reviews:
- **Scope**: Comprehensive / Focused / Exploratory
- **Year range**: Filter by publication years
- **Sections**: Introduction, Current State, Key Findings, Gaps, Future Directions
- **Output**: Structured review with citations in APA/MLA/Vancouver/Chicago format

---

## 8.3 Paper Tab
Write research papers in IMRaD structure:
- **Format**: APA, MLA, Vancouver, Chicago, IEEE
- **Word count**: Configurable
- **Sections**: Abstract, Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion
- **TTS**: Read aloud with Kokoro/Edge-TTS/Browser 3-tier fallback

---

## 8.4 Thesis Tab
PhD/PG thesis chapter writing:
- **Chapters**: Introduction, Literature Review, Methodology, Results, Discussion, Conclusion
- **Branch**: General Pharma, Clinical Pharmacy, Pharmacology, Medicinal Chemistry, Pharmacy Practice
- **Degree**: PhD, M.Pharm, M.S.

---

## 8.5 Grant Writer Tab
Generate grant proposals:
- **Agency**: NIH, DST, ICMR, NSF, ERC, etc.
- **Budget**: Configurable amount and currency
- **Duration**: Project timeline
- **Sections**: Abstract, Specific Aims, Background, Methods, Timeline, Budget Justification, Expected Outcomes

---

## 8.6 Regulatory Tab
FDA/EMA guideline search:
- **Agency**: FDA, EMA, ICH
- **Checklists**: NDA, ANDA, MAA, IND
- **Search**: Query regulatory databases

---

## 8.7 Citation Manager Tab
Collect, organize, and export citations:
- **Import**: Manual entry, from Literature tab, from DOI
- **Export styles**: APA, Nature, AMA, Vancouver, BibTeX
- **Projects**: Organize citations by project
- **Deduplication**: Automatic DOI-based dedup

---

## 8.8 Lecture Tab
Generate lecture content:
- **Duration**: 30-120 minutes
- **Level**: Undergraduate / Graduate / PhD
- **Output**: Learning objectives, content sections, discussion questions, homework

---

## 8.9 Slides Tab
Generate presentation slides:
- **Style**: Academic, Clinical, Corporate, Minimal
- **Count**: 3-30 slides
- **Citations**: Include/exclude references
- **Output**: Structured slide content ready for PowerPoint

---

## 8.10 Example: Writing a Systematic Review
1. Click **Lit Review** tab
2. Enter topic: "CRISPR delivery mechanisms for in vivo gene therapy"
3. Set scope: Comprehensive, years 2020-2026
4. Select sections: Introduction, Current State, Key Findings, Gaps, Future
5. Click **Generate** — receive structured review with 50+ citations
6. Click **Refine** to send to agent for polishing
7. Click **Download** to save as TXT

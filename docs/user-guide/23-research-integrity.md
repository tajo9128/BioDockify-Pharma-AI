# Chapter 23: Research Integrity & Ethical Use

## 23.1 Hijacked Journal Detection
- Cross-reference against `hijacked_journals.json` database
- Alert if journal name matches known hijacked journals
- Provide authentic URL for verification

---

## 23.2 Fake Website Detection

6 checks for journal website authenticity:
1. **Domain reputation** — Known publisher vs suspicious domain
2. **Free TLD detection** — .tk, .ml, .ga, .cf, Blogspot, Wix
3. **ISSN registry URL** — Match against portal.issn.org
4. **Crossref validation** — ISSN registered in Crossref
5. **Domain age** — Very new domains suspicious
6. **Hijacked journal DB** — Match against known clones

## 23.5 AI-Generated Content Disclosure

### When to Disclose
- Literature reviews generated by AI
- Statistical analysis suggestions
- Paper drafting assistance
- Figure generation

### How to Disclose
```markdown
## AI Assistance Disclosure
Portions of this manuscript were drafted with assistance from 
BioDockify Pharma AI (v6.9.5). All content was reviewed and 
verified by the authors.
```

## 23.6 Limitations of AI in Pharma Research

| Limitation | Mitigation |
|-----------|-----------|
| QSAR predictions not experimental | Validate with wet lab |
| Docking scores are estimates | Use as ranking, not absolute |
| Literature synthesis may miss papers | Manual review recommended |
| Statistical suggestions need validation | Verify assumptions |

---

## 23.7 Responsible Use Guidelines

1. **Verify all predictions** with experimental data
2. **Document limitations** in methods section
3. **Disclose AI assistance** in acknowledgments
4. **Follow institutional guidelines** for AI use
5. **Maintain human oversight** for critical decisions
6. **Preserve data integrity** with backups
7. **Respect intellectual property** of training data
8. **Report failures** to improve the system

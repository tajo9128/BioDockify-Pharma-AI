## Competitor Landscape

### Tier 1 — Direct Online Tool Competitors

#### 1. PyRx (pyrx.sourceforge.io)
- **Type:** Downloadable free tool (not cloud)
- **Strengths:** Most widely cited in academic papers, huge user base, free
- **Weaknesses:** Requires local install, no batch, no AI scoring, dated UI, no MD
- **SEO:** Strong backlinks from academic citations; ranks for "molecular docking software"
- **Our angle:** "Do what PyRx does, in your browser, with AI scoring — no installation"
- **Key comparison page:** `/compare/biodockify-vs-pyrx` ✅ already exists — optimize it

#### 2. SwissDock (swissdock.ch)
- **Type:** Free online docking server
- **Strengths:** Academic credibility (Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics), free, no install
- **Weaknesses:** No batch, no MD, no ADMET, slow queue, limited customization
- **SEO:** Strong .ch domain, academic backlinks
- **Our angle:** Full pipeline (dock → MD → ADMET) vs single-step tool

#### 3. DockingServer (dockingserver.com)
- **Type:** Online docking service
- **Strengths:** Online, no install
- **Weaknesses:** Paid per docking, no pipeline, limited scoring options
- **Our angle:** Integrated pipeline, AI consensus scoring, better value

### Tier 2 — Enterprise / Desktop Competitors

#### 4. Schrödinger / Maestro / Glide
- **Type:** Enterprise commercial suite (~$50k+/year)
- **Strengths:** Industry gold standard, FEP+, comprehensive workflows
- **Weaknesses:** Prohibitively expensive, steep learning curve, enterprise sales process
- **SEO:** Massive domain authority, ranks for everything in the space
- **Our angle:** "Enterprise-grade results without the enterprise price"
- **Comparison opportunity:** `/compare/biodockify-vs-glide` ✅ exists → optimize

#### 5. UCSF Chimera / ChimeraX
- **Type:** Free visualization + basic docking tool
- **Strengths:** Free, well-known, academic standard for visualization
- **Weaknesses:** Not primarily a docking tool, complex interface, no cloud
- **Our angle:** We handle the docking; use Chimera for visualization if you want — or use our built-in viewer
- **Comparison page:** `/compare/biodockify-vs-chimera` ✅ exists → optimize

#### 6. AutoDock 4 / AutoDock Vina
- **Type:** Free open-source command-line tools
- **Strengths:** Gold standard algorithms, free, peer-reviewed
- **Weaknesses:** Command-line only, no GUI, complex setup, no pipeline
- **Our angle:** "We run AutoDock Vina under the hood — with a UI, batch support, and AI scoring"
- **Note:** Vina comparison in blog ✅ — needs dedicated `/compare/` page

### Tier 3 — Emerging AI Competitors

#### 7. Boltz-2 / DiffDock / NeuralPLexer
- **Type:** AI-native docking tools (open source)
- **Strengths:** State-of-the-art AI scoring, rapidly improving
- **Weaknesses:** Require GPU infrastructure, no UI, research-only maturity
- **Our angle:** We can integrate and expose these models via our platform

#### 8. Orion (OpenEye/Cadence)
- **Type:** Cloud-based commercial platform
- **Strengths:** Enterprise cloud, full workflow
- **Weaknesses:** Enterprise pricing, complex sales
- **Our angle:** Accessible pricing, open platform

## Executive Summary

BioDockify is an AI-powered molecular docking and drug discovery platform competing in a niche with high-intent, low-volume search traffic. The site already has **107 indexed pages**, a deep blog (60+ posts), a knowledge hub, and comparison pages — a strong technical foundation. The primary gaps are:

1. **Zero recorded backlinks** — the single biggest ranking limiter
2. **Wrong positioning in Google's eyes** — Search Console shows Google indexing you for "pharma AI writing platform" queries, not computational chemistry tools
3. **No solutions/audience pages** — content exists but isn't organized by who uses it (grad students, pharma researchers, CROs)
4. **Comparison pages underdeveloped** — only 4 exist; 20+ opportunities identified

The strategy focuses on three pillars: fix Google's understanding of the site, win long-tail computational chemistry keywords, and build backlinks through the academic community.

## Backlink Strategy (Critical Priority)

BioDockify has no recorded backlinks. This is the #1 ranking blocker.

### Fast Wins (weeks 1-8)
1. **Submit to tool directories**: AlternativeTo, G2, Capterra (free listings), SourceForge, Product Hunt
2. **Academic repositories**: Submit to bio.tools (ELIXIR registry for bioinformatics), Galaxy ToolShed, Bioconductor (if applicable)
3. **GitHub presence**: Create an open companion repo (e.g., example docking workflows), link back to BioDockify
4. **Zenodo/Figshare**: Publish a dataset or benchmark study citing the platform
5. **ResearchGate**: Create institutional profile with tool citation

### Medium Term (months 2-4)
6. **Guest posts** on Towards Data Science, Towards AI, Medium/Science publications (computational biology angle)
7. **Reach out to GROMACS/AutoDock tutorial sites** — offer to be listed as a tool that implements their software
8. **University lab pages** — contact computational biology labs offering free access for citing BioDockify in methods sections
9. **Protocol exchange** (Springer Nature) — publish a protocol paper: "Cloud-based molecular docking workflow using BioDockify"

### Long Term (months 4-12)
10. **Peer-reviewed publication** — methods paper in Journal of Cheminformatics or JCIM is the highest-value link in this niche
11. **Conference submissions** — ACS, AAPS, ISOP computational sessions
